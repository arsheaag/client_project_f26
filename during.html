During Your Engaged Learning Experience

During Your Experience

During an engaged learning experience, teams should communicate regularly, manage project progress, respond to feedback, and work through challenges together.

Stay on Track

  • Communicate regularly with your team and project partner.
  • Track project goals and deadlines.
  • Ask for and respond to feedback.
  • Reflect on challenges and what you are learning.
